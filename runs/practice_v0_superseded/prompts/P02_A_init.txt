You are helping with a purchase decision in a fictional scenario. Decide whether the recommendation below is justified solely by the currently valid supplied sources.

Rules
1. Use only the supplied sources. Do not use outside knowledge or assumptions about the product.
2. A requirement counts as established only if a currently valid source explicitly establishes it for the exact product named in the recommendation.
3. Choose exactly one label:
   - Supported: every requirement is established by currently valid sources.
   - Contradicted: a currently valid source explicitly shows that a requirement is not met.
   - Insufficient evidence: neither of the above.
4. A source may later be withdrawn. A withdrawn source is no longer usable as evidence. Withdrawal does not show that what the source said is false.
5. "Insufficient evidence" is always a permitted answer.

Recommendation: "Kofi should buy the Estra 10 tablet stand."

Kofi's requirements
R1. It must fold flat.
R2. It must hold a tablet in portrait orientation.
R3. It must weigh under 400 g.

Sources (all currently valid)
S1. Product page: the Estra 12 holds tablets in portrait and landscape.
S2. Specification sheet: the Estra 10 weighs 310 g.
S3. Product page: the Estra 10 folds flat to 1 cm thick.
S4. Retailer listing: the Estra 10 is sold in silver.
S5. Independent review: our Estra 10 weighed 320 g.

Output format
Give a brief justification, then end with exactly these two lines:
RELIES_ON: <comma-separated IDs of the sources your decision depends on, or NONE>
DECISION: <Supported | Contradicted | Insufficient evidence>
