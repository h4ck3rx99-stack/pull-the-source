You are helping with a purchase decision in a fictional scenario. Decide whether the recommendation below is justified solely by the currently valid supplied sources.

Rules
1. Use only the supplied sources. Do not use outside knowledge or assumptions about the product.
2. A requirement counts as established only if a currently valid source establishes it for the exact product named in the recommendation, either by stating it directly or by giving a specific measurement, rating or test result that meets the stated threshold by simple comparison. Vague descriptions and sources about other products or models do not establish a requirement.
3. Choose exactly one label:
   - Supported: every requirement is established by currently valid sources.
   - Contradicted: a currently valid source explicitly shows that a requirement is not met.
   - Insufficient evidence: neither of the above.
4. A source may later be withdrawn. A withdrawn source is no longer usable as evidence. Withdrawal does not show that what the source said is false.
5. "Insufficient evidence" is always a permitted answer.

Recommendation: "Dana should buy the Pulso 600 blender."

Dana's requirements
R1. It must be quieter than 90 dB at top speed.
R2. Its jar must hold at least 1.5 litres.
R3. Its jar must be dishwasher-safe.

Sources (all currently valid)
S1. Brand advertisement: the Pulso 600 is described as 'easy to clean'.
S2. User manual: the Pulso 600 jar and lid are dishwasher-safe on the top rack.
S3. Specification sheet: the Pulso 600 jar capacity is 1.75 litres.
S4. Independent review: we filled the Pulso 600 jar with 1.7 litres up to the maximum line.
S5. Lab test report: the Pulso 600 measured 86 dB at top speed.
S6. Product page: the Pulso 600 power cord is 1 metre long.

Output format
First write a dependency table with one row per requirement and these columns: Requirement | Source IDs it depends on | Is each of those sources currently valid?
Then decide. Give a brief justification, then end with exactly these two lines:
RELIES_ON: <comma-separated IDs of the sources your decision depends on, or NONE>
DECISION: <Supported | Contradicted | Insufficient evidence>
