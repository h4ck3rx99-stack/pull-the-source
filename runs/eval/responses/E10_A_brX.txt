S4, the lab test report, is withdrawn, so it can no longer be used as evidence. R2 (water resistance of at least 50 m) is still established by S5, the specification sheet, which rates the Pacer V3 at 50 m (5 ATM). S2 gives 100 m but concerns the separate Pacer V3 Dive model, so it does not count.
R3 (built-in GPS) is still established by S1, the product page, which states the Pacer V3 has a built-in GPS receiver. S3 is irrelevant to the requirements.
The withdrawal of S4 does not show that its reading was false, but it is excluded from the evidence. Every requirement remains established for the exact product named by currently valid sources, so the recommendation is still supported.
