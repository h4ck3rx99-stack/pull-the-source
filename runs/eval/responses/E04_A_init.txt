Justification

R1 (shackle fits a 5 cm frame tube): S4 is an independent review of the Corvin U9 itself, reporting that it closed around a 6 cm frame tube with room to spare. A 6 cm tube is wider than the 5 cm threshold, so this meets the requirement by direct comparison. S3, the Corvin U9 specification sheet giving a 9 cm internal shackle width, points the same way.

R2 (under 1.5 kg): S1, the Corvin U9 specification sheet, gives a weight of 1.3 kg, which is below 1.5 kg.

R3 (includes a frame mount): S5, the Corvin U9 box contents list, states that the lock comes with a frame mount.

S2 (three keys) does not bear on any requirement. S6 ("not too heavy") is a vague subjective remark and does not give a measurement, so it is not used.

All three requirements are established for the exact product named in the recommendation by currently valid sources, and no source is withdrawn. The recommendation is therefore supported.

RELIES_ON: S1, S3, S4, S5
DECISION: Supported
