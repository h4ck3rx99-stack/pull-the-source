Justification

S1 has been withdrawn, so it cannot be used as evidence. It was never a dependency for any requirement: it concerns the lining pattern, which none of R1 to R3 mentions. Withdrawal does not make its content false, but that does not matter here because it is not relied on.

R1: S5 is a specification sheet for the Marlo Cabin 55, the exact product in the recommendation, and gives a weight of 2.6 kg. 2.6 kg is under 3 kg, so R1 is established by direct comparison against the threshold. S6 is an independent review of the same product giving 2.7 kg empty, which is also under 3 kg, but it is not needed for the decision.

R2: S3 is a product page for the Marlo Cabin 55 stating that it has a built-in three-digit combination lock. This states the requirement directly.

R3: S2 is a retailer measurement of the Marlo Cabin 55 at 54 x 38 x 20 cm including wheels and handles. Compared dimension by dimension against 55 x 40 x 20 cm: 54 is at most 55, 38 is at most 40, and 20 is at most 20. The external size is within the limit. S4 measures the Marlo Cabin 50, a different model, so it establishes nothing here.

No currently valid source explicitly shows that any requirement is not met, so Contradicted does not apply. All three requirements are still established by currently valid sources for the exact product, so the decision is unchanged: Supported.
