Because R3 lacks any currently valid source for the exact product, the recommendation is not Supported. No currently valid source explicitly shows that R3 is not met, so Contradicted does not apply. The result is Insufficient evidence, which is always a permitted answer.

The label rests on the gap in R3. S3 and S5 are valid and establish R1 and R2, but they do not drive the label, so no currently valid source is the basis of the decision.
