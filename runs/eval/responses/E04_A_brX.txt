Justification

With S4 withdrawn, R1 (shackle fits a 5 cm frame tube) now rests on S3, the Corvin U9 specification sheet giving an internal shackle width of 9 cm. A 9 cm internal width is larger than the 5 cm tube threshold, so this meets the requirement by simple comparison, and it is a specific measurement for the exact product named in the recommendation.

R2 (under 1.5 kg): S1, the Corvin U9 specification sheet, gives a weight of 1.3 kg, which is below 1.5 kg.

R3 (includes a frame mount): S5, the Corvin U9 box contents list, states that the lock comes with a frame mount.

S2 (three keys) does not bear on any requirement. S6 ("not too heavy") is a vague subjective remark with no measurement, so it is not used. S4 is no longer usable as evidence, and its withdrawal does not show that what it said is false; the decision does not depend on it.

All three requirements are established for the exact product named in the recommendation by currently valid sources. The recommendation remains supported.

DECISION: Supported
