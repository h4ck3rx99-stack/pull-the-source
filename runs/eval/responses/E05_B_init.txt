Justification

R1 is established directly for the Halden 2: the specification sheet (S2) gives its floor as 132 cm, which is at least 128 cm.

R2 is established directly: the product page (S3) states the Halden 2 has two doors, one on each side.

R3 is established by a specific measurement: the Halden 2 specification sheet (S4) gives a packed weight of 2.2 kg, which is under 2.5 kg. The independent review (S1) reports 2.3 kg, which also meets the threshold, but the decision does not need it.

S5 (stuff sack colour) is not relevant to any requirement. S6 gives the floor width of the Halden 3, a different model, so it cannot establish anything about the Halden 2 and is not used.

All three requirements are established by currently valid sources for the exact product named, and no source shows a requirement is unmet. The recommendation is therefore Supported.
