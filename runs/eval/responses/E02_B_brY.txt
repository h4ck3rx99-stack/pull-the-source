Decision

S1 has been withdrawn, so it can no longer support R2. That withdrawal does not show that the 1.08 kg figure is false, but the rules do not allow it to be used. R2 is still established, because S6 gives a specific measurement for the exact product (1.05 kg) that is under 1.2 kg by simple comparison. S6 is currently valid, and nothing says it has been withdrawn. R1 (S4) and R3 (S2) are unchanged, and both remain established by currently valid sources. S3 ("laptop-friendly") is vague and is not relied on. S5 concerns a sternum strap and is irrelevant to the requirements. Every requirement is still established by currently valid sources, so the recommendation remains supported.
