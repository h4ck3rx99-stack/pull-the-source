Justification

R1 is established directly for the exact product named. S5 states that the Sonvia H40 includes a 3.5 mm audio cable and has a 3.5 mm jack on the left ear cup. S1 is not used because it concerns the earlier Sonvia H30, which the rules exclude.

R2 is established directly. S6 states that the Sonvia H40 ear cups fold flat into the headband, which meets the stated requirement to fold for travel.

R3 is established by a specific measurement that meets the threshold by simple comparison. S3 reports 35 hours in an independent battery test, and S4 reports 38 hours in a lab test. Both are at or above 30 hours. Either source alone is sufficient, and both are listed because both establish the requirement.

S2 (the companion app's equaliser) relates to no requirement, so it does not bear on the decision.

No source is withdrawn, and every cited source is stated as currently valid. Every requirement is therefore established, so the recommendation is supported. This is a finding of support only; it is not a claim that the product is the best choice.
