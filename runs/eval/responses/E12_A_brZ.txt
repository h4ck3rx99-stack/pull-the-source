Justification:

Withdrawing S3 changes nothing. S3, the carry-handle listing, was never evidence for any of Tomas's requirements, so the decision rests on the same sources as before.

R1 (shuts off when tipped over) is established for the Calda Mini by S6, the user manual. The Calda Tower tip-over protection in S5 belongs to a different model and cannot count toward this requirement.

R2 (draws at most 1500 W) is established by S1, which rates the Calda Mini at 1200 W on its highest setting. That is below the 1500 W limit. The measured 1180 W in S2 is consistent with this but is not needed.

R3 (adjustable thermostat) is established by S4, the product page, which lists an adjustable thermostat dial on the Calda Mini.

Every requirement is still established for the exact product by currently valid sources, and no currently valid source shows that a requirement is unmet. The recommendation remains supported.
